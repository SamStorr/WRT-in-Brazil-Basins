How the scoring works

Water in basin plans assesses whether climate and water risks are understood and adequately considered

Water in sub-national development planning and governance assesses whether governance systems and institutions are able to respond

Water and climate connections in specific sectors asks if water and climate decisions are connected across sectors that depend on water

Links to climate financing and project implementation asks if plans can be translated into fundable action

The scores reflect the proportion of questions that were answered as fully, partially or not included in the plans
